How it works

From road footage
to useful context.

Each step adds evidence. A road hazard only reaches the public map after human review.

01◉

Capture

A rider records video with timestamped location readings, then uploads after stopping safely.

02⌁

Review

Computer vision will suggest possible potholes. A reviewer checks the evidence and approximate location.

03↗

Inform

Only verified hazards will appear on the public map and support nearby audio alerts.
